# APROPOS SEARCH VISIBILITY & SEO AUDIT

Date: August 22, 2026
Status: CONTROLLED WORKING AUDIT
Primary hub: https://marketplace.aproposgroupllc.com
Organization: APROPOS Group LLC

## Executive determination

The APROPOS ecosystem has a solid technical SEO foundation on its procurement and business-development properties, but search visibility is fragmented across domains and the entity relationship between APROPOS Group LLC, the Marketplace, and the production services is not yet expressed consistently enough.

The highest-value work is not mass keyword-page generation. The priority is to make every intended public page crawlable, canonically stable, internally linked, semantically distinct, and clearly associated with the APROPOS parent entity and the correct operational service.

## P0 findings

1. Marketplace build-time sitemap ownership is incomplete.
   - `scripts/apply-seo-production.cjs` regenerates `sitemap.xml` on every production build.
   - The build-time ROUTES registry does not currently include every committed public route. `nat-corp-contract-matching` is present in the committed sitemap but not in the generator.
   - Any new property marketing pages would therefore be omitted from the generated sitemap unless the route registry is extended.

2. Marketplace public landing-page presentation needs higher legibility.
   - Existing public SEO landing pages use white/light-gray page fields and white cards.
   - The user reports these pages appear blurry.
   - Corrective direction: solid APROPOS navy/sapphire surfaces, crisp light text, Georgia headings, Arial body copy, no translucent blur filter, no decorative shadow treatment.

3. AI4 Businesses lacks committed crawl-control files.
   - The production repository does not currently contain a root `robots.txt` or `sitemap.xml`.
   - Add both and include only public canonical URLs intended for search.

4. AI4 Website Design English and Spanish need stronger page-level metadata consistency.
   - English and Spanish root pages need explicit self-canonicals, descriptions, social metadata, and structured data where absent.
   - The language pair should use reciprocal `hreflang` annotations.
   - English and Spanish process claims should be synchronized; the sampled source currently describes seven questions in English and six in Spanish.

## P1 findings

5. Marketplace needs dedicated property/service pages distinct from the existing generic search-intent pathways.

Create substantive public pages for:
- Registered Federal Contractors Portal
- NAT-CORP Contract Exchange
- National Enterprise Business Center (NEBC)
- AI4 Businesses
- AI4 Website Design Studio
- AI4 Website Design Studio — Español
- APROPOS Group LLC

Each page should answer:
- What is this service?
- Who is it for?
- What problem does it solve?
- What does it provide?
- How does the customer journey work?
- What are the current commercial terms, where applicable?
- What does it not guarantee?
- Where is the operational production site?

6. Marketplace internal-link architecture should use two layers.
   - Generic intent pages: federal opportunities, state/local opportunities, contract fit, readiness, proposal development, partnerships.
   - Property pages: deeper explanation of the APROPOS production service.
   - Primary CTA: operational production property.
   - Secondary CTA: Marketplace deep-dive page.

7. Corporate entity graph should include the complete current public ecosystem.
   - Current corporate remediation already connects RFCP, NAT-CORP, NEBC, and Marketplace.
   - AI4 Businesses and the English/Spanish AI4 Website Design properties should also be represented with the semantically correct relationship (WebSite/Service/Brand rather than claiming a separate legal organization unless that is factually correct).

## Search visibility observation

A sampled public web search on August 22, 2026 consistently surfaced the APROPOS corporate domain for branded ecosystem queries, but did not consistently surface separate results for all individual production domains. This is a visibility signal, not proof of non-indexing. Search Console URL Inspection and performance data should remain the authoritative index/status measurement when available.

## GEO / AI-search direction

Do not create special AI-only files or speculative schema. Use the same fundamentals required for search:
- crawlable public HTML;
- strong internal links;
- explicit, stable entity names;
- unique and useful service explanations;
- visible facts that match structured data;
- clear limitations and disclaimers;
- source-backed regulatory/statistical claims;
- good desktop/mobile page experience.

The new Marketplace property pages should be written in an answer-first format that gives AI systems and human visitors the same clear facts: identity, audience, service, workflow, terms, limitations, and destination.
