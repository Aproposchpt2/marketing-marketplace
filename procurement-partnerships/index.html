Partnerships That Expand Procurement Participation

Procurement participation grows when business-support organizations, economic-development groups, institutions, and contractor networks share clear pathways to opportunity.

Who this is for

A practical starting point

This page is for chambers, business centers, APEX and small-business assistance organizations, economic-development groups, institutions, associations, and procurement-support partners.

The Marketplace role

APROPOS Marketing Marketplace explains the pathway and routes visitors onward. Operational services are provided at the destination site identified on this page.

The need

Why this pathway matters

Opportunity information can fail to reach capable businesses, while support organizations may lack a consistent way to route participants toward discovery, readiness, matching, and proposal resources.

Outreach alignment

Connect opportunity information with businesses and communities that may otherwise be missed.

Service referrals

Route participants to the federal, state/local, readiness, matching, or proposal pathway that fits.

Program coordination

Clarify audience, geography, responsibilities, communications, and measurable objectives.

The APROPOS pathway

From discovery to the right destination
