More Ways to Participate in Contract Opportunity

Not every business needs to begin as a prime contractor. Some may contribute as subcontractors, suppliers, specialty firms, local partners, or members of a broader performance team.

Who this is for

A practical starting point

This page is for businesses exploring prime, subcontractor, supplier, and teaming roles, and for organizations seeking broader contractor participation.

The Marketplace role

APROPOS Marketing Marketplace explains the pathway and routes visitors onward. Operational services are provided at the destination site identified on this page.

The need

Why this pathway matters

Businesses can overlook viable roles when they treat every opportunity as an all-or-nothing prime bid. At the same time, participation requires a real connection between the work, qualifications, capacity, and contractual responsibilities.

Prime contractor

Take responsibility for the complete requirement when capability, experience, and capacity support it.

Subcontractor or supplier

Perform a defined portion of work or provide necessary goods within another contractor's delivery plan.

Team participation

Combine complementary capabilities where the solicitation and contracting structure permit it.

The APROPOS pathway

From discovery to the right destination

What to expect

A clearer next decision

Visitors should expect to describe their capabilities, confirm the role they can perform, review opportunity-specific requirements, and evaluate whether a prime, subcontract, supplier, or partnership route is appropriate.
