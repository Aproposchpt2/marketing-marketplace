Website → Capability Profile

Your public business evidence becomes a useful starting point.

Your website may explain more about your business than a procurement search box can capture. Nat-Corp organizes that evidence into a Business Capability Profile reflecting products or services, specialties, markets, and other relevant business evidence.

It is a starting profile—not an unchangeable conclusion.

Customer verification

You remain the authority on your business.

Nat-Corp gives the customer an opportunity to verify, correct, or refine the profile before it becomes the basis for opportunity matching. This helps prevent an old webpage, incomplete service list, or misunderstood phrase from controlling the results.

Review before matching

Confirm services and products. Add missing capabilities. Remove outdated information. Clarify markets and coverage. Then approve the profile for opportunity evaluation.

Capability-matched contract discovery

Qualified opportunities are evaluated for business alignment.

Nat-Corp compares qualified released public-sector opportunities with the verified profile. The goal is not to show every notice. It is to help the business identify which opportunities appear more relevant and deserve a closer look.

Business context

Ranked attention

Frequently asked questions

What businesses and partner organizations should know.

Is Nat-Corp another contract search database?

No. It starts with the business, builds a profile, asks the customer to verify it, and evaluates qualified opportunities against that profile.

Do I need procurement codes before I start?

No. Begin with basic information and the business website.

What if the website is incomplete?

The customer can review and edit the profile before matching.

Does Nat-Corp apply for contracts?

No. It supports discovery and matching. The business reviews requirements and decides whether to respond.

Does a high match mean the business will win?

No. Alignment is not a guarantee of eligibility, responsiveness, selection, or award.

Can counseling organizations refer businesses?

Yes. Nat-Corp is an additional referral resource and complement to counseling.

What is the resident-state view?

It focuses on qualified opportunities in the business’s home state while preserving an all-state option.

How does 30-day partner access work?

An eligible business uses an approved code supplied by a participating partner. This page does not publish a live code.
