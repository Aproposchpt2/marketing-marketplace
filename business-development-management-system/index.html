The platform

Less time searching. More time advising.

The Advisor Contract Search Portal centralizes public-sector opportunity research so Advisors do not have to monitor numerous procurement websites independently. It brings Los Angeles County public-sector opportunities and nationwide Federal Government opportunities into one searchable business-development environment.

The Portal was developed around the work of Business Development Advisors: identify an opportunity that appears relevant to a client's capabilities, research it efficiently, and bring it directly into the advisory conversation.

More than 1,000 active opportunities aggregated across Los Angeles County Departments, municipal cities, Public Works agencies, school districts, colleges, universities, transportation authorities, utilities, special districts, joint powers authorities, and other public agencies and entities.

Federal Government

Structured Search

Search by industry, service category, agency, contract category, NAICS classification, solicitation type, geography, posting date, and closing date as applicable to the opportunity source.

Frequently asked questions

What Agencies should know.

Is this a contractor self-service portal?

No. The primary operating model is Agency and Advisor centered. Advisors use the Portal to research opportunities for the businesses they serve.

Does it replace government procurement portals?

No. The originating government agency or authorized procurement publisher remains the authoritative source for official solicitation documents, amendments, registration requirements, deadlines, forms, and bid submission.

Can one license be shared with unlimited staff?

No. The Professional Agency License includes up to five authorized users. Each Advisor account should be assigned to an individual user associated with the subscribing Agency.

Are more BDMS modules planned?

Yes. The Advisor Contract Search Portal is the first completed module of the broader Business Development Management System. Additional modules may be introduced as the platform expands.

Is there an annual commitment?

No. The current Professional Agency License is month-to-month.

What is the value proposition?

Reduce procurement-research time, improve opportunity visibility, and give Advisors another practical business-development capability they can bring into the client relationship.
